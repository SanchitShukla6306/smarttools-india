Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to SmartTools India. This Privacy Policy explains how our website and its tools may handle information when you visit or use our services.

1. Information We Process

SmartTools India does not currently require users to create an account. Information entered into calculators, such as age, percentage and EMI values, is intended to be processed in your browser. Please avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into any online tool.

2. Image Compressor

The image compressor uses browser-based image processing. Images selected for compression are processed in your browser by the tool. Do not use this statement if your implementation uploads images to a server or third-party service.

3. QR Code Generator

The QR Code Generator may use an external QR code service to create QR images. Text entered into this tool may be sent to that service. Do not enter passwords, private documents, financial details or other sensitive information.

4. Currency Converter

The currency converter may request exchange-rate information from an external API. Your browser connects to that service to retrieve rates. The rates are for reference and may not match the rate offered by a bank or currency provider.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember preferences, such as dark mode. This is different from a server account. Your browser settings may allow you to clear locally stored website data.

Some features may rely on external services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
